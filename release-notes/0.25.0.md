# etcds 0.25.0

Not published yet. The features `auth` and `approvals` need signon 1.1.0.

## New

- **Leases.** `LeaseGrant`, `LeaseRevoke`, `LeaseKeepAlive`, `LeaseTimeToLive` (with its keys)
  and `LeaseLeases`. A lease is granted, kept alive and expired on one node, the leader, on one
  clock; a put with a lease works on any node, and a revoked or expired lease's keys are
  deleted.
- **Locks.** `Lock` and `Unlock`, etcd's layout (`name/<lease hex>`): queued per name on the
  leader, first come first served; the holder's lease ending frees the lock.
- **One leader decides** leases, locks and sequences for the cluster: the `leader` an embedder
  sets, else the lowest node id. A call on another node is sent on to it.
- **What the leader decides is recorded before it is answered** - a lease, a lock's holder, a
  sequence's next number - on a majority of the nodes, or in a store the embedder gives. A new
  leader takes the records over under a ballot of its own, so a number handed out or a lock
  granted is not lost by a change of leader, and a leader that was superseded cannot answer.
- **Sequences.** Cluster-wide numbers that never repeat: a node takes a block from the leader
  (`seq_next`, `seq_take`, `seq_define` for an embedder), and any etcd client takes numbers by
  a put on the queue `etcds-seq` and a watch on its reply queue.
- **Auth** (feature `auth`, with `auth_db_url`): etcd's Auth API on signon's accounts.
  `Authenticate` is signon's sign-in and its token a signon session; roles are signon's roles,
  a role's permissions its key ranges. Every KV, Txn, Watch, Lease and Lock call is checked
  against them, and the role `root` manages it.
- **Approvals of a queue's messages** (feature `approvals`, with `auth`): a queue that has an
  approval definition is gated - a message put to it waits until enough different accounts
  approved it, and then goes into the queue as any other. The votes are collected on a key,
  the message's state, which anyone allowed may read and watch. Who may what is signon's
  approval scale by labac: comment 2, recommend 3, approve 4, veto 5 (it blocks every approval
  and drops the ones collected), override 6 (it lifts a veto). See `approval.md`.
- **`peer_token`**: a secret every node shares, on each call one node makes to another. With
  auth it is required: a peer's header alone is one any client can send.
- **Alarms** (`Maintenance.Alarm`: get, activate, deactivate), kept on each node and shown by
  `status`.

## Changed

- `Lease`, `Lock` and `Auth` answered `unimplemented` before; `Election` still does.

## Known gaps

- Without an embedder's `leader`, a network split may have a leader on each side.
- A revoked role or session works on for up to 5 seconds on a node that checked it just before.
- Alarms are each node's own, not raised cluster-wide.
